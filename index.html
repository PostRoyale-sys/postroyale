Make a match, post the link on X — everyone who opens it drops in with you. New round every 8 minutes. No install, no account.

How a match works

  1. Drop. Glide down onto the island — steer with WASD.
  2. Loot. Open chests (E) in houses and under trees: weapons, ammo, shields, medkits.
  3. Harvest & build. Pickaxe trees, rocks, cars and houses for wood, brick and metal — then build walls, floors and ramps.
  4. Survive the storm. The circle shrinks — outside it you take damage.
  5. Win. Last one standing gets the Victory Royale.
